# 🚀 From the Trench to Tech: Launching GrabenGeist

I used to build roads with hot asphalt and heavy machinery. Now, I build the software that keeps those sites safe.

🚧 **The Reality**: Construction sites are chaotic. Mud, noise, and split-second decisions. As a certified *Strassenbauer*, I saw how easily safety protocols could slip through the cracks of a legacy paper trail.

💻 **The Solution**: Introducing **GrabenGeist**.

It's not just another dashboard. It's a digital guardian for trench work. I built it to detect hazards that tired eyes might miss—translating onsite chaos into clear, actionable alerts.

**Key Features:**
*   🌩️ **Red Lightning Alerts**: Immediate visual cues for critical safety breaches.
*   🛡️ **Lint-Free & Secure**: Built with strict security protocols (A+ standards).
*   📱 **Field-Ready**: Designed for the people actually doing the work.

This journey from manual labor to **Software Product Owner** taught me one thing: The best tools aren't built in a bubble. They are built by someone who knows what it feels like to wear the gloves.
