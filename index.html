🚀 The Shipping

A project on a local drive helps no one. I prioritize deployment and usability, ensuring my tools work on the devices they were meant for, from job site tablets to desktop workstations.

ⵣ Awal Dictionary

PWA Cultural Tech

A comprehensive digital dictionary for the Tamazight language (Zaouiat Ahansal dialect). Built as a high-performance PWA that works offline and installs on any device.

Flip for Details

Case Study

  • Offline Capable (PWA)
